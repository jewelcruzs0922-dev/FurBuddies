# Product Image Provenance

**Current imagery (v3): AI-generated.**

All 24 product images and 8 collection images in this portfolio demo are **AI-generated
photorealistic product photographs** produced with the Google Gemini image model
(`gemini-2.5-flash-image`) from descriptive text prompts, then cropped to square 1:1 and
resized to 1000×1000.

- Each image depicts the product named in its title (e.g., a kraft pouch of dog kibble,
  a cotton rope toy, a stainless bowl, a teal collar).
- Prompts explicitly excluded text, logos, branding, and people, so no third-party
  trademarks or fabricated certifications appear.
- No third-party copyright content is used, so no external attribution is required.
- These are synthetic images for a fictional, non-transactional portfolio store; they do
  not depict real products for sale.
- The homepage hero image (`furbuddies-hero.png`), the editorial "Our promise" image
  (`furbuddies-editorial.png`), and the Dogs/Cats collection card images are likewise
  AI-generated (wide/portrait variants framed so the subject is not cropped).
- Each of the 24 products also has a **second, AI-generated "lifestyle" image** (a dog or
  cat eating or using the product) shown on card hover, generated with the same model and
  the same no-text/no-logo constraints.
- The brand logo and favicon (`furbuddies-logo.png`, `furbuddies-logo-header.png`,
  `furbuddies-favicon.png`) were supplied by the store owner; the white background was
  keyed to transparent.

### Prompt template used

> Photorealistic e-commerce product photograph: `<product description>`, centered on a
> seamless warm light-beige studio background, soft diffused natural lighting, subtle soft
> shadow, shallow depth of field, square 1:1 composition. No text, no logos, no branding,
> no people, no watermark. Looks natural and realistic, like a real e-commerce product photo.
